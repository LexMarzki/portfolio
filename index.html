The Company

A trusted technology partner for serious projects.

Marzki is an independent IT consulting firm based in Malaysia. We work with businesses that need more than off-the-shelf software — partners who want a thoughtful, engineering-led approach to building the platforms that power their growth.

We specialize in delivering web applications, integrated business systems and modernization initiatives across industries including finance, logistics, retail and professional services. Every engagement is led directly by senior practitioners — no handoffs, no layers, no inflated teams.

Our model is intentionally lean. That means faster decisions, clearer communication and a sharper focus on the outcomes that matter to your business.
